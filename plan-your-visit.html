Getting There

Getting to Liwa

Liwa sits in the Al Dhafra region, roughly 2.5 hours by car from Abu Dhabi city and around 3.5 hours from Dubai. The festival grounds are signposted from the E11 and E65 highways.

By car

Follow the E11 south-west toward Tarif, then the E65 (Liwa Road) directly into the Al Dhafra region. On-site parking is signposted at the main gate, with a Parking Pass add-on available at checkout for reserved bays.

By taxi or ride-hail

Taxis are available from Abu Dhabi city and Madinat Zayed; journey time is similar to driving. We recommend booking a return journey in advance during peak headline evenings (NYE and closing night).

On-Site Camping

Camping at Liwa

A dedicated camping zone with shared amenities, lighting, and 24-hour security patrol runs for the full festival period. Reserve a Camping Pass directly during ticket checkout — no separate app download required.

Before You Go

Travel & weather tips

☀️

Desert weather

Daytime highs of 24–28°C, cool evenings around 12–15°C in December.

💧

Hydration

Carry a refillable bottle — free water refill stations are placed across the grounds.

🚙

4x4 recommended

A 4x4 is recommended for dune-side parking and any guided desert tour add-on.

🏨

Book accommodation early

Rooms near Liwa fill quickly for NYE and closing weekend — see Stay & Dine.

🧥

Layer up at night

Desert evenings cool quickly — bring a light jacket for after-dark stages.

🌵

Respect the environment

Stick to marked tracks and take litter with you — the dunes are a protected landscape.

🏰

Visit the forts

Combine your trip with a self-guided tour of nearby historic watchtowers — see Experience Liwa.

🗣️

Local experiences

Arabic and English are both widely spoken across festival staff and vendors.

Trip Planning FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there parking on-site?

Yes — general parking is free and signposted; a reserved Parking Pass add-on is available at checkout.

Can I bring my own tent?

On-site camping requires a booked Camping Pass; independent tents are not permitted for safety and security reasons.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are not permitted on festival grounds, with the exception of registered falconry animals during scheduled shows.

Is there mobile signal at the festival grounds?

Yes, though coverage can be intermittent in the dunes — download your e-ticket in advance from the Tickets page.

What if an event is sold out?

Sold-out sessions are clearly marked on the Tickets page — join the waitlist via the Feedback page and we'll notify you of releases.
