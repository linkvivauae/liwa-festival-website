Our Commitment

Accessibility statement

Liwa International Festival is committed to making this website and our festival grounds usable by the widest possible audience, including visitors using assistive technology.

  • Pinch-to-zoom is enabled sitewide; no viewport restrictions are applied.
  • All meaningful images carry descriptive alternative text.
  • Every page uses a single, clear heading hierarchy starting with one H1.
  • Interactive controls — search, filters, the itinerary panel, and the ticketing steps — are keyboard-operable with visible focus states and ARIA labelling.
  • Animations respect the operating system's "reduce motion" setting.
  • On-site, accessible parking, ramped walkways, and a quiet room are available near the main gate.

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this site or on the festival grounds, please tell us via the Feedback page and we will address it as a priority.
